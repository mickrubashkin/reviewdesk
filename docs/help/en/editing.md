# Edit the text of an email

Region managers and board owners edit the emails of the boards they work on. Edit the emails of your own region.

ReviewDesk never changes the original HTML. You change the text fields of the email (subject, preheader, headings, paragraphs, buttons), and ReviewDesk builds the final HTML from them.

## Edit a field

1. Open the email.
2. Select text in the email and click **Edit**.
3. Change the text. The toolbar adds bold, italics, links, subheadings and lists. **Insert Bitrix variable** puts in a placeholder that Bitrix24 fills on send.
4. Save. Each save makes a new version.

A field linked to a **shared text** says so. Changing it there changes it in every email that uses it; unlink it to give this email its own text.

An email **built from blocks** shows its blocks in **Details → Blocks**. A change to a block changes every email that uses it.

## Undo a change

**History** lists every version with what changed. **Restore version** brings an older one back as a new version.

## Mark the email ready

The **Draft** / **Ready** badge in the header is a menu for region managers and board owners.

You cannot mark an email ready while it has open **blocking** comments or a required approval area is not approved. The menu says what is blocking it.

When an email becomes ready, a CRM operator pastes it into Bitrix24: see [Bitrix24 upload](bitrix24-upload.md).

## Other actions (region managers, board owners)

In the **More** menu of the review page:

- **Duplicate as…** — a copy as another language, region or version.
- **Archive email** — take it off the board. It stays in the **Archive** view and can be restored to its place.
