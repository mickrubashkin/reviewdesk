# Board views

Switch views with **View** in the board header. The view is part of the link, so a link you send opens the same view.

## Timeline (default)

The way of a deal through the pipeline. Stages are the nodes of the trunk; each stage's emails branch off it, with the days counted from the deal entering that stage. There is no time scale between stages: each stage counts on its own.

- One branch per language, or, on a board with regions, one per region with the reference first. **timing differs** on an email means its region waits longer or shorter there than the reference; hover it to see the reference's wait.
- **Highlight** picks out the emails that need something: **To upload**, **Open comments**, **Not live** or **Outdated**.
- **Copy link** on a stage gives a link that opens the board at that stage.
- **Present** shows the sequence without the review tools, to walk someone through it: no statuses, comments, highlight or filters, just the emails, their days and the waits between them. Click an email to see it large. The link with present mode on opens the same for anyone who can see the board; **Exit present** brings the tools back.

## Cards

Stages as columns, one card per email. Good for scanning statuses and comment counts.

## Matrix

Slots as rows (grouped by stage), languages or regions as columns. It shows which email goes out in each language and where the gaps are.

Region managers and sequence owners can also:

- edit a slot as a whole (name, place, timing for all languages),
- drag a row to reorder slots or move one to another stage,
- select cells, rows or stages and change many emails at once (status, archive),
- fill an empty cell with **Create from EN**.

## Archive

Emails taken off the board: who did it, when, and how often the portal saw them sent. Region managers and sequence owners can restore an email to its old place.

## New slot, new email (region managers, sequence owners)

**New → New slot (all languages)** adds a place in a stage: build it from blocks, paste the HTML from a robot, or take emails the portal has seen sent. **New → New email** adds one email to an existing slot.
