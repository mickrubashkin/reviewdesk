# Comment and mention a colleague

Anyone who can sign in can comment on any email.

## Comment on a piece of text

1. Open the email.
2. Select the words in the email you want to talk about.
3. Click **Comment** in the small toolbar, write, and send.

To comment on the email as a whole, open **Comments** in the side panel and add a comment there.

Choose the kind of comment:

- **Blocking** — must be fixed. The email cannot be marked ready while a blocking comment is open.
- **Suggestion** — nice to have; does not block.

## Mention someone

Type `@` and a name. The person sees the comment in **My tasks → Mentions**.

## Reply and resolve

- Reply in the thread under the comment.
- **Resolve** when it is done. The comment's author, region managers and board owners can resolve it.
- **Hide resolved** and **Only open comments** keep the list short.
- **Next with open comments** jumps to the next email of the board that still has open comments.

Comments refresh every few seconds; you do not need to reload the page.
