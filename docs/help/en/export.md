# Export

**Export…** is in **Settings** (region managers and board owners) or in the account menu (everyone else).

## Which emails

- **Whole board**,
- **Current filters** — what the filters show now,
- **Selected** — what is selected in the **Matrix**.

## How

- **Board table** (Excel or CSV) — one row per email in board order: slot, timing, language, status, subject, owner, link.
- **JSON handoff** — the list with statuses and counts, for scripts and other tools.
- **HTML (zip)** — the final HTML of each email, as it goes into Bitrix24. Region managers and board owners.
- **Markdown for AI (zip)** — the copy, links, notes and comments of each email, plus one combined file. Region managers and board owners.

A single email: **More → Download HTML** on its review page.
